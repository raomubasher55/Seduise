FAQ – Seduice

1. What is Seduice?

Seduice is a creative platform that allows you to generate personalized erotic stories in text and audio formats. Whether you enjoy romance, fantasy, or more intense scenarios, Seduice adapts to your desires.

2. Is the content really personalized?

Yes. You can customize characters, themes, and situations. The story is dynamically generated based on your choices and evolves accordingly.

3. How does the audio feature work?

Once your story is generated, you can choose to create an audio version narrated by a seductive AI-generated voice. Each audio corresponds to the full story.

4. Do I need to be 18 to use Seduice?

Yes. Seduice is strictly for users aged 18 or older. Age verification is required when entering the site.

5. What data do you collect?

We only collect essential data: your email address, first name (optional), and age (for age verification). This is used to create your account and personalize your experience.

6. How does the subscription work?

Seduice offers different subscription plans based on the number of stories or audio tracks you want to generate. You can cancel your subscription anytime from your user dashboard.

7. Can I use Seduice for free?

We occasionally offer free trials or limited access to explore the platform. However, advanced features such as audio generation are only available through subscription.

8. Can I request a refund?

Due to the personalized nature of the content, no refunds are issued once a story or audio has been generated. For technical issues, please contact support@seduice.com.

9. Is my content private?

Yes. Your stories and audio are strictly private and only accessible from your account. We do not share any content without your permission.

10. How can I delete my account?

You can delete your account from your dashboard. All data will be permanently removed in accordance with our data policy.

11. What languages are supported?

Currently, Seduice supports story generation in English only. More languages will be added in future updates.

12. My question isn’t listed here, what should I do?

Contact us at support@seduice.com — we’ll be happy to help you.
