Feature Development Milestones – Story App
Timeline: 2 Months
Total Budget: $250
Date: July 2025

Milestone 1: Story Continuation
•	• Allow users to continue a story over multiple chapters.
•	• Chapters are unlocked based on the user's plan or by purchasing extra credits.
Budget: $50
Time: Week 1–Week 2

Milestone 2: Interactive Story Choices & Improved Reading Experience
•	• At the end of each chapter, users can choose between multiple scenarios to make the experience more interactive.
•	• Adjustable text size and font
•	• “Reading mode” with a clean, immersive layout (like an e-reader)
•	• Soft color palette to enhance intimacy and focus during reading
Budget: $30
Time: Week 3

Milestone 3: Audio Generator Update
•	• Replace the current audio engine with a more affordable alternative without compromising on quality
•	• Improve loading speed and voice synthesis clarity
Budget: $30
Time: Week 4

Milestone 4: Credits and Subscription Plans Management
•	• Implement a credit system to generate chapters or enable audio
•	• Allow users to buy additional credits once their monthly quota is used up
•	• Tiered subscription plans:
•	•   - Free: up to 3 chapters / no audio
•	•   - Standard ($4): up to 5 chapters / limited audio
•	•   - Premium ($22): up to 10 chapters / full audio / exclusive content access
Budget: $35
Time: Week 5–Week 6

Milestone 5: Premium Story Gallery
•	• Create a dedicated section with premium stories
•	• Only paying subscribers can access this space
•	• Premium users get early or exclusive access to new content before it’s available to others
Budget: $20
Time: Week 6

Milestone 6: Visibility & Community Engagement
•	• Users can choose to make their stories public or private
•	• Public stories can be viewed, liked, or voted on by other subscribers
•	• Top authors can earn badges or community rewards based on their engagement
Budget: $20
Time: Week 7

Milestone 7: Final Testing, QA & Launch Prep
•	• Conduct thorough testing of all features
•	• Fix bugs, optimize UI/UX, and prepare deployment
•	• Collect beta user feedback and polish remaining issues
Budget: $35
Time: Week 8
